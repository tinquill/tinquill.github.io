QR Pop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

The short version. QR Pop has no servers and no accounts. Everything you scan and everything you create stays on your phone — we never receive it. The only data that leaves your device is what Google components send to Google: the advertising SDK, to show ads; ML Kit, as technical diagnostics; and, only if you switch it on, crash reports. None of them ever includes what you scanned or created.

Who we are

QR Pop is an Android app published by Tinquill. You can reach us at inscorp.dev@gmail.com.

What you scan

  • Camera frames are analysed on your phone by Google ML Kit, bundled inside the app. A frame exists only for the instant it takes to look for a code, then it is discarded. Frames are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Scanning an image you pick or share with QR Pop works the same way: decoded on your phone, never uploaded. Picking a photo uses Android's photo picker and needs no storage permission.
  • QR Pop does not check links against any server. It inspects the link's structure on your phone and shows its real destination. When you tap Open, the link opens in your browser, which applies its own safety checks.
  • QR Pop never opens a link, dials a number, or sends a message by itself — only when you tap the action.

Scan history

  • History is stored in QR Pop's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read, and is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer.
  • Wi-Fi passwords in history are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM). The key is created on your device and cannot be exported.
  • Two-factor authentication setup codes and driving-licence barcodes are never saved.
  • No location is recorded with any scan.
  • You choose how long history is kept, and can delete any item or all of it at any time. Uninstalling QR Pop removes it. We hold no copy, so we cannot recover it.

Codes you create

QR codes are generated on your phone. They leave your device only when you save them to your gallery or share them, to a destination you choose. Saved images are ordinary files that other apps with photo access can read.

What we collect

Nothing. Tinquill operates no servers for QR Pop, has no accounts, and QR Pop contains no analytics software — we receive no data about you, your scans, or your use of the app.

Crash reports (off unless you switch them on)

QR Pop includes Firebase Crashlytics, disabled by default. If you turn on Settings → Send crash reports, a report is sent to Google Firebase when the app crashes: the stack trace, device model, Android version, app version, and a per-installation identifier. Reports never include scan contents, history, or created codes. You can switch it off again at any time.

What Google ML Kit sends for diagnostics

Even though scanning happens on your phone, ML Kit sends Google a small amount of technical data for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypted in transit:

  • Device information (manufacturer, model, Android version)
  • App information (package name and version)
  • A per-installation identifier, which Google states is not intended to identify you or your device
  • Performance and error metrics, such as processing time and error codes

This never includes camera images or barcode contents.

What Google's advertising SDK collects

QR Pop is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. Ads never appear on the camera or on a scan result. To show and measure ads, Google's SDK collects data directly — it does not pass through us:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)Serving and measuring ads, limiting repeats
Approximate location, derived from IP addressShowing region-appropriate ads
Device and app informationAd compatibility and fraud prevention
Ad interaction events (views, clicks)Measuring ad performance

This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses data from partner apps. Ads never see what you scan or create.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, QR Pop shows Google's consent form before any ad loads, and you can change your choice in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • In US states with their own privacy laws, Google's consent platform honours applicable opt-out signals.

Permissions QR Pop uses

PermissionWhy

QR Pop does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, or photo library. Saving a contact, joining Wi-Fi, or adding a calendar event hands the details to Android's own screen, where you confirm.

Children

QR Pop is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and similar laws you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. Tinquill holds none. For data held by Google, exercise those rights through Google.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Open-source licences

QR Pop is built on open-source software. The licences are listed in the app under Settings → Licences.
