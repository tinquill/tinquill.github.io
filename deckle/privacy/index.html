Deckle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

The short version. Deckle has no servers and no accounts. Your scanned documents are encrypted and stay on your phone — we never receive them, and we could not read them if we wanted to. Deckle includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own. The only data that leaves your device is what two Google components send to Google: the advertising SDK, to show ads, and ML Kit, as technical diagnostics. Neither ever includes your documents or their text. Both are described below.

Who we are

Deckle is an Android app published by Tinquill. You can reach us at inscorp.dev@gmail.com.

Your documents

Everything you scan stays in Deckle's private storage on your device.

  • Page images are encrypted using a key held in the Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM). The key is generated on your device and cannot be exported from it.
  • Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on your phone using on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit, bundled with the app — no page is ever uploaded for processing). Extracted text stays in the same on-device, encrypted store as the images.
  • Document titles, extracted text, and folder names never leave your device.
  • Files are stored in app-private storage, which other apps cannot read, and are excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.
  • A document leaves your device only when you explicitly share or export it, to a destination you choose.

Uninstalling Deckle — or deleting documents individually from within the app — permanently removes them from your device. Because the data never reaches us, we cannot recover it for you and have nothing to delete on our end.

How scanning and barcode capture work

Deckle does not request Android's Camera permission itself, and none of the following sends an image anywhere off your device:

  • Document scanning uses the ML Kit Document Scanner, a component of Google Play services. It runs as its own screen with its own camera access; Deckle never sees a raw camera feed, only the cropped page image it hands back.
  • Barcode / QR capture opens your device's own Camera app to take a single photo, the same way any app can ask for a photo. Deckle decodes the barcode from that photo on-device (again with ML Kit) and does not keep the photo once decoding is done.

App lock

Deckle's optional biometric app lock (fingerprint/face, via Android's own BiometricPrompt) is enforced entirely on your device. No biometric data is ever collected, stored, or seen by Tinquill — Android itself handles the match and only tells Deckle whether it succeeded.

What we collect

Nothing. Tinquill operates no servers for Deckle, has no accounts, and Deckle contains no analytics or crash-reporting software of its own — we receive no data about you, your documents, or your use of the app.

What Google ML Kit sends for diagnostics

Deckle's scanning, text recognition and barcode features use Google ML Kit. Even when the processing itself happens on your phone, ML Kit sends Google a small amount of technical data for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypted in transit (HTTPS):

  • Device information (manufacturer, model, Android version, ML hardware)
  • App information (package name and version)
  • A per-installation identifier, which Google states is not intended to identify you or your device
  • Performance and error metrics, such as processing time, image format and size, and error codes

This never includes your page images, extracted text, barcode contents, or filenames. Google states that ML Kit does not transfer this data to third parties.

What Google's advertising SDK collects

Deckle is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's SDK collects data directly — it does not pass through us:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)Serving and measuring ads, limiting repeats
Approximate location, derived from IP addressShowing region-appropriate ads
Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)Ad compatibility and fraud prevention
Ad interaction events (views, clicks)Measuring ad performance

This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Google's use of advertising data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads never see your documents. No scan, no extracted text, and no filename is shared with Google or any advertiser.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops personalised ads across all apps.
  • Change your consent at any time in Deckle under Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices. This row only appears if consent withdrawal legally applies to you (currently the EEA, UK and Switzerland — see below).

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland

Before any ad loads, Deckle shows a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where you choose whether to allow personalised ads. Your choice is stored on your device and can be changed at any time from Deckle's Settings screen.

If you are in a US state with its own privacy law

Google's User Messaging Platform also recognises applicable US state privacy signals (for example, opt-out-of-sale/share signals under laws like the CCPA/CPRA) and adjusts the ads Deckle is served accordingly, the same way it does for EEA/UK consent.

Children

Deckle is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. The app is rated for a general audience.

Data retention

Your scans remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. We hold no copy and therefore have nothing to retain or delete on your behalf. For advertising data held by Google, use the Advertising ID controls above or contact Google directly.

Your rights

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and similar laws you have rights to access, correct, and delete your personal data. Because Tinquill holds none, there is nothing to request from us — your documents are already exclusively in your possession. For data held by Google as an advertising provider, exercise those rights through Google.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
