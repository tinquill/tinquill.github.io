Deckle

Scan documents to PDF in seconds.

Point your camera at a page. Deckle finds the edges, straightens it, cleans it up, and saves a sharp PDF you can share anywhere.

Offline No account No watermark

What it does

  • Automatic edge detection and perspective correction
  • Multi-page scanning — build a whole document in one session
  • Filters: original colour, greyscale, high-contrast black & white
  • Search the text inside your scans, recognised on your device
  • Export as PDF or JPEG, share to any app
  • Folders, rename, sort
  • Optional fingerprint lock

Privacy

Deckle has no servers and no accounts. Scans are encrypted on your device and never uploaded. Full detail in the privacy policy.
